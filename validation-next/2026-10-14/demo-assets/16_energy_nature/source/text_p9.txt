Product detection and analysis
Detection. The gas products and liquid products were quantiﬁed by
online gas chromatography and 1H nuclear magnetic resonance spec-
troscopy (1H-NMR, Bruker 400 M), respectively. The 0.2 mM Dimethyl
sulfoxide (DMSO, Aladdin, 99.8 %) diluted in D2O (Energy Chemical,
99.9%) was used as an internal standard for 1H-NMR, and a water
suppression mode was also adopted.
